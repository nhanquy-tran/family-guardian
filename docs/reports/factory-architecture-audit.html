Factory architecture audit

A principal-architect review of the factory after the MVP build: execution logs since the day-2 review, the Zühlke AI Factory blueprint, and the stacked reference architecture of OpenSpec (what), obra/superpowers (how) and OpenHands (where). Suggestions are costed and prioritised; nothing here has been built yet.

Verdict

The factory is strong where it is deterministic: tests-first with red/green evals, a separate test writer and arbiter, evidence per gate, migration dry runs and version checks, a watchdog. It is weak where state and truth live outside versioned artefacts: OpenSpec stops at propose (no verify, no archive, no source-of-truth specs), orchestration lives in one Claude session, and gate fixes often bypass the gates. Since the day-2 review the factory became markedly more reliable; the next gains come from closing the spec lifecycle and moving orchestration state into the repo, not from more agents.

Takeaway: on the "where" layer GitHub Actions already gives most of what OpenHands offers (ephemeral sandboxes, issue-to-PR, watchdog). The real gaps are in the "what" layer (OpenSpec lifecycle) and the "how" layer (per-task discipline, debugging), plus durable orchestration state.

Recommendation

  • Do step 1 now (1.1, 1.2, 1.3 first). It closes the biggest drift risk (no source of truth), removes the orchestrator as a single point of failure and costs almost nothing.
  • Do step 2 selectively: 2.3 two-stage review, 2.4 systematic debugging and 2.5 hidden acceptance tests give the most verification value; 2.2 per-task TDD adds cost and turns on the most expensive agent, so trial it on two items first.
  • In step 3, do 3.1 and 3.2; they fix "previews share the production DB" and restore Zühlke rule 5. Treat 3.3 (OpenHands) as a learning experiment: GitHub Actions already provides the sandbox and issue-to-PR loop, so the gain is mainly 24/7 operation without this session and multi-container parallelism.
  • Expected outcome: run cost rises from about $1.70 to $2.20–2.50 per item, while interventions should drop below one per five items and the factory can run at step 05 for low-risk changes.
